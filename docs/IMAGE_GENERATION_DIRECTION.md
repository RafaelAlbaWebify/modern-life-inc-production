# Generated Image Direction — Modern Life Inc.

## Master visual language

Editorial psychological illustration for a faceless YouTube channel.

Core traits:
- dark charcoal / near-black background
- warm ivory human figures
- amber/orange focal accents
- clean modern editorial composition
- simplified human forms
- subtle geometric environments
- symbolic visual metaphors
- high contrast
- no photorealism
- no visible brand logos
- no embedded text unless explicitly requested
- composition optimized for 16:9 video
- negative space reserved for overlays when useful

## Character consistency

Characters should feel like members of the same visual universe, but do not need to be exact recurring individuals.

Prefer:
- simple silhouettes
- minimal facial detail
- expressive posture
- clean wardrobe shapes
- readable gestures

Avoid:
- hyper-detailed faces
- uncanny realism
- cartoon mascot look
- childish stick figures
- glossy 3D

## HERO images

Use stronger composition and metaphor.

Examples:
- two people separated by a field of ambiguous signals
- eye contact represented as intersecting light paths
- two speech streams gradually syncing
- multiple small signals converging toward one shared point

## HYBRID images

Use simpler compositions with clear overlay zones.

Examples:
- two people in a café with space above for labels
- one person leaning in while the other responds
- a phone/message scene with room for arrows/icons

## Consistency rule

All generated visuals should look like they could appear in the same issue of one editorial magazine.
