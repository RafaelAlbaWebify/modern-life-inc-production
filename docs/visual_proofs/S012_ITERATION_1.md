# Visual Proof Iteration 1 — S012 Mutual Eye Contact

## What worked

- Strong charcoal / amber / ivory palette.
- Clear focal relationship between two people.
- Immediate readability at a glance.
- Warm emotional tone without explicit sexuality.
- 16:9 composition works for video.
- The gaze connection is visually legible.

## What did not work

- Too detailed for the intended Modern Life Inc. system.
- Feels closer to cinematic romance / graphic-novel illustration than editorial psychology.
- Characters are too individualized and attractive-looking, which risks making the channel feel like dating fiction rather than behavioral analysis.
- Background has too much visual information for repeated episode production.
- The dashed gaze arrows and circular highlights are baked into the image; overlays should normally be added by the compositor.
- Harder to combine consistently with SYSTEM scenes because of the rendering/detail gap.

## Direction for iteration 2

Reduce detail and move toward:
- editorial illustration;
- simplified human forms;
- flatter shape language;
- less facial detail;
- more negative space;
- more graphic composition;
- environment suggested, not fully rendered;
- generated base image without baked-in arrows/text;
- compositor adds gaze lines, labels and highlights separately.

## Rule learned

Generated HERO/HYBRID images should enrich the channel without becoming a different visual universe from SYSTEM scenes.

A HERO image can be richer than a SYSTEM scene, but should still share:
- silhouette language;
- palette;
- edge treatment;
- abstraction level;
- proportion style.

## Decision

Iteration 1 is **not** the final style reference.

Use it as an example of the upper bound of detail we should avoid.
