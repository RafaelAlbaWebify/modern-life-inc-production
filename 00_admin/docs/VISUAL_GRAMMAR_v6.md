# Modern Life Inc. — Visual Grammar v6

Benchmark principle: clarity and simple limited animation.

1. One visual idea per scene.
2. Dave must remain recognizably the same character.
3. Prefer flat cartoon shapes, thick clean outlines and limited shading.
4. Avoid infographic overload.
5. Avoid duplicating narration with large on-screen text.
6. Most scenes should last roughly 2–5 seconds.
7. Use hard cuts as the normal transition.
8. Animation vocabulary:
   - pose swap
   - prop/state swap
   - slide in/out
   - scale pop
   - small rotation
   - visibility on/off
   - simple number/value change
9. Avoid constant Ken Burns motion.
10. Full-frame generated comic/storyboard sheets are not video assets.
11. Scene composition is authored before rendering.
12. Reusable assets are only used when they maintain quality and character consistency.
