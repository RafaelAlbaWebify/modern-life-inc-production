# Modern Life Inc. — Channel OS

Canonical operating repository for the YouTube channel **Modern Life Inc.**

## Positioning

Modern Life Inc. explains the hidden psychology behind money, attraction, status, work and everyday human behavior.

**Brand promise:** Understand people. Understand modern life.

## Editorial mix

- Relationships & social psychology: 50%
- Money psychology: 25%
- Status & work: 15%
- Biases & self-control: 10%

## Canonical storage

- **GitHub:** strategy, research, scripts, episode metadata, metrics snapshots, automation and lightweight assets.
- **Google Drive:** only heavy or irreplaceable production binaries.
- **YouTube / vidIQ:** channel analytics and market intelligence.

## Storage rule

Keep only what is necessary to operate, reuse or audit. Anything reproducible is disposable.
