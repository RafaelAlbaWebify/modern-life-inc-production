## Launch-order note

Raw total score is not the only decision rule.

MLI-001 is chosen from relationships because:
1. strongest direct competitor/outlier evidence;
2. broad emotional appeal;
3. low production complexity;
4. high chance of generating early browse data;
5. establishes the human-behavior promise before introducing money psychology.

Recommended early sequence:
1. attraction / hidden signals
2. chasing / distancing
3. money psychology / lifestyle creep
4. respect / social status
5. money + status bridge

This sequence tests whether the same audience can travel across the channel's intended pillars.
