# Positioning

## Core idea

Modern Life Inc. explains human behavior in modern life through psychology, social dynamics and money behavior.

## Audience promise

Help viewers understand why people behave the way they do — in relationships, money, status, work and consumption.

## We cover

- Relationships & attraction
- Communication & charisma
- Social status & power
- Behavioral finance / money psychology
- Consumer behavior
- Work psychology
- Cognitive biases & self-control

## We do not cover

- Stock picks
- Daily market news
- Crypto news
- ETF rankings
- Pure macroeconomic news
- Generic dating manipulation content
- Sexualized clickbait
- Repetitive AI-template content
