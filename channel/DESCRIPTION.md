Modern Life Inc. explores the hidden psychology behind money, attraction, status, work and everyday human behavior.

Why do we chase people who pull away? Why does earning more rarely feel like enough? Why do some people command respect without saying much? And why do we spend money trying to impress people we barely know?

Through simple visual stories, psychology and real-world examples, we break down the forces shaping how we think, spend, connect and compete.

Understand people. Understand modern life.
