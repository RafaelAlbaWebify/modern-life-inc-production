# Editorial Rules

1. Every video must answer a concrete human question.
2. Prefer real-life framing over academic terminology.
3. Every script needs a thesis, evidence/examples, counterpoint where useful, and takeaway.
4. Never publish scripts that are only list-template variations.
5. Attraction content must avoid manipulation framing.
6. Money content must remain behavior-first, not markets-first.
7. Prefer evergreen topics.
8. A video must fit the same viewer who watches our other pillars.
9. Automation may reduce production cost, never originality.
10. Claims about psychology or finance must be sourced proportionately to their importance.
