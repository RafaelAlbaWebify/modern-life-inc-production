# Brand System

## Name

Modern Life Inc.

## Tagline

Understand people. Understand modern life.

## Visual direction

- Dark charcoal background
- Warm ivory figures/text
- Warm amber/orange accent
- Simple human silhouettes
- Minimal diagrams, arrows, icons and labels
- High-contrast thumbnails
- No complex character rigging

## Colors

- Near black: #0F0F0F
- Charcoal: #2A2A2A
- Ivory: #F4EFE6
- Amber: #FFB020
- Orange: #FF7A00

## Thumbnail principle

One idea, one emotional conflict, one visual hierarchy.
Keep text short and readable at mobile size.
