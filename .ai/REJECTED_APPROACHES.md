# Rejected Approaches

- Art-Deco/cut-paper showroom renderer: too complex and weak for repeatable automation.
- Character-left/prop-right auto-layout: repetitive PowerPoint-like result.
- Generic white UI/card prop containers: looked like app widgets.
- Constant Ken Burns movement: cosmetic/robotic.
- Infographic-heavy generated scenes: too much text and visual clutter.
- Motion-comic/storyboard-sheet pan: v5 dead end; visually unacceptable.
- Composite character images with embedded props: caused duplicate objects and blocked clean state animation.
- Alpha transparency as the only asset-QC criterion: insufficient.
- Scaling to full episode before 15s/45s benchmark passes: wastes effort.
