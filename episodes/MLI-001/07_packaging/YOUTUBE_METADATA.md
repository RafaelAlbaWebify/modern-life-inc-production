## Description
You finally get a raise. Your salary goes up. And somehow your bank account barely notices.

This episode of Modern Life Inc. explains the difference between nominal wages and real purchasing power, why your personal cost of living can feel very different from the headline inflation rate, and how housing, insurance, debt, taxes and other essential costs can absorb a pay increase before you ever feel richer.

The dollar figures used in Dave's example are illustrative. They are not national averages or financial advice.

Sources include the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, OECD Employment Outlook 2026 and the Federal Reserve's latest household economic well-being report.

Modern Life Inc. — satirical economics for modern survival.
