# MLI-001 — Creative Brief

## Working title

**7 Quiet Signs Someone Is More Attracted to You Than They Let On**

## Core promise

Help the viewer distinguish between random friendliness and a *pattern* of behaviors that may indicate attraction, without pretending any single sign proves how another person feels.

## Why this video first

- The topic has both search demand and strong browse/outlier evidence.
- It is emotionally relevant to a broad audience.
- It can be gender-neutral.
- It supports a simple visual language.
- It lets the channel establish credibility by avoiding manipulative certainty.

## Editorial angle

Not:
> "If they do X, they definitely want you."

Instead:
> "Single cues are ambiguous. Repeated, converging behaviors are more meaningful."

The episode should repeatedly distinguish:
- signal vs proof;
- one-off behavior vs pattern;
- personality baseline vs change in behavior;
- attraction vs friendliness.

## Target structure

1. Hook: the problem with trying to read one signal.
2. Sign 1 — repeated proximity / opportunities to be near you.
3. Sign 2 — attention that persists beyond politeness.
4. Sign 3 — remembering unusually specific details.
5. Sign 4 — behavior changes around you versus their normal baseline.
6. Sign 5 — reciprocal self-disclosure / conversation investment.
7. Sign 6 — low-risk bids for future interaction.
8. Sign 7 — clusters of signals rather than isolated cues.
9. Counterpoint: anxiety, extroversion, friendliness and culture can mimic attraction.
10. Takeaway: look for consistency, reciprocity and change from baseline.

## Production target

- 6–9 minutes
- simple micro-scenes
- major visual refresh roughly every 6–10 seconds
- no character rigging
- high-contrast dark/ivory/amber visual system
- minimal on-screen text
