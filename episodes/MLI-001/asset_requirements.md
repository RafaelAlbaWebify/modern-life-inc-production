# MLI-001 Asset Requirements

## Reusable human poses

Create a neutral unisex figure system first.

Required poses:
1. standing neutral
2. standing engaged / leaning slightly forward
3. standing withdrawn / turned away
4. sitting neutral
5. sitting engaged
6. walking
7. pointing / explaining
8. phone in hand
9. listening
10. thinking

Each pose should work mirrored horizontally.

## Face/head assets

- profile head left
- profile head right
- front head
- eye/gaze left
- eye/gaze right
- neutral
- interested
- uncertain

No lip sync.

## Backgrounds

Only simple reusable environments:
- café / casual social setting
- office / workplace
- neutral room
- abstract dark gradient

Avoid detailed bespoke backgrounds unless they add meaning.

## Asset budget rule

Do not generate a unique image for every scene.

Target:
- 10 human poses
- 15–20 icons
- 4 backgrounds
- 10 layout templates

Those should cover the entire episode through recombination.
